# infrastructure.cost.anomaly-detection - slice property-floor

Policy-grade generic baseline practice, not repository-tailored Context or Evidence coverage.

The `property-floor` slice holds ownership at `cost.anomaly-detection` and prevents none from being silently absorbed. It activates `anomaly-detection-source-code-boundary`, `anomaly-detection-evidence-receipt` and reports a named coverage gap when its repository binding or required dependency is unavailable.
