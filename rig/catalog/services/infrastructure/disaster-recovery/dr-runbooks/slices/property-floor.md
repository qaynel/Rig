# infrastructure.disaster-recovery.dr-runbooks - slice property-floor

Policy-grade generic baseline practice, not repository-tailored Context or Evidence coverage.

The `property-floor` slice holds ownership at `disaster-recovery.dr-runbooks` and prevents none from being silently absorbed. It activates `dr-runbooks-source-code-boundary`, `dr-runbooks-evidence-receipt` and reports a named coverage gap when its repository binding or required dependency is unavailable.
