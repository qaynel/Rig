# infrastructure.disaster-recovery.rto-rpo - slice property-floor

Policy-grade generic baseline practice, not repository-tailored Context or Evidence coverage.

The `property-floor` slice holds ownership at `disaster-recovery.rto-rpo` and prevents none from being silently absorbed. It activates `rto-rpo-source-code-boundary`, `rto-rpo-evidence-receipt` and reports a named coverage gap when its repository binding or required dependency is unavailable.
