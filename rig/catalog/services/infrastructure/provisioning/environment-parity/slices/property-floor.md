# infrastructure.provisioning.environment-parity - slice property-floor

Policy-grade generic baseline practice, not repository-tailored Context or Evidence coverage.

The `property-floor` slice holds ownership at `provisioning.environment-parity` and prevents none from being silently absorbed. It activates `environment-parity-source-code-boundary`, `environment-parity-evidence-receipt` and reports a named coverage gap when its repository binding or required dependency is unavailable.
