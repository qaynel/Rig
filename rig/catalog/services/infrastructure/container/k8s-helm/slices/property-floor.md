# infrastructure.container.k8s-helm - slice property-floor

Policy-grade generic baseline practice, not repository-tailored Context or Evidence coverage.

The `property-floor` slice holds ownership at `container.k8s-helm` and prevents none from being silently absorbed. It activates `k8s-helm-source-code-boundary`, `k8s-helm-evidence-receipt` and reports a named coverage gap when its repository binding or required dependency is unavailable.
