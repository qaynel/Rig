# testing.regression.suite-curation - slice property-floor

Policy-grade generic baseline practice, not repository-tailored Context or Evidence coverage.

The `property-floor` slice holds ownership at `regression.suite-curation` and prevents none from being silently absorbed. It activates `suite-curation-source-code-boundary`, `suite-curation-evidence-receipt` and reports a named coverage gap when its repository binding or required dependency is unavailable.
