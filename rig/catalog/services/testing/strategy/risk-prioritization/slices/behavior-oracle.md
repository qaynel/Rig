# testing.strategy.risk-prioritization - slice behavior-oracle

Policy-grade generic baseline practice, not repository-tailored Context or Evidence coverage.

The `behavior-oracle` slice requires an example that distinguishes a conforming risk prioritization result from a real policy violation. It activates `test-priority-ranking-source-code-boundary`, `test-priority-ranking-strategy-context` and reports a named coverage gap when its repository binding or required dependency is unavailable.
