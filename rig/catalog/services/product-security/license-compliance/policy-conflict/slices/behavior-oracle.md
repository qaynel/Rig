# product-security.license-compliance.policy-conflict - slice behavior-oracle

Policy-grade generic baseline practice, not repository-tailored Context or Evidence coverage.

The `behavior-oracle` slice requires an example that distinguishes a conforming policy conflict result from a real policy violation. It activates `policy-conflict-source-code-boundary`, `policy-conflict-license-compliance-context` and reports a named coverage gap when its repository binding or required dependency is unavailable.
