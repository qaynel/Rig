Simulate threats in a contained environment with declared scenarios. Customer records and unapproved production targets do not belong in adversarial experiments.

Grade: minimal. Assurance remains Policy grade. Example: ["malformed token"].

Name scenarios that apply to this system and identify the exact isolated deployment and assets they may touch. A configured scope is not proof that the test runner is isolated.
