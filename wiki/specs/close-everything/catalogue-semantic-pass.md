pass over every service identity: each service now states its own minimal, mid
and maximal policy reason, and its own coverage ceiling, in language specific to
what that service's declared fields can and cannot show. Repeated frames were
removed rather than reworded: any non-machine sentence that three or more other
services also carried (after identifier normalisation) was deleted, so no
sentence is shared by more than three services, and the machine-checked lines
(grade declaration, check IDs, `Disposition:`, acceptance target, declared
predicates) stay because the installed matrix and the authorship gate read them.

**Disposition for every row: executable at the Policy assurance ceiling.** No
service was upgraded to a Context or Evidence claim, and no executable
integration was invented to make documentation look stronger. The ceiling
column is the honest limit a reviewer should hold the service to.
