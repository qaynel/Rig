## Why

The frozen case reuses one `approval` object. `executePlan` checks the in-memory
`approval.used` flag first (`rig/lib/lint-format.js:459`), so the second call is
rejected before the durable on-disk one-use record (`consumePlanApproval`,
`:483`) is ever consulted. The case therefore proves less than its title
claims: it still passes with the durable protection deleted.
