Per-component, evidence-backed; whole-repo only when everything covered (recommended). A component is "supported" only when Rig genuinely built at least the Policy level for it, discovered and bound its commands, and produced a real check result — not a placeholder — under approved consent. The whole repository is "supported" only when every discovered, non-excluded component clears that bar. Any exclusion you approved suppresses the whole-repository claim, while the components that are covered stay truthfully supported. This is the convergence of everything you've already frozen: partial coverage, no false green, truthful reporting.
